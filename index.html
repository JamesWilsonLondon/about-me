What I do

I place software engineers at startups — Seed through Series D, mostly London-based or remote-first UK companies. I take a small number of roles at a time and go deep. The goal is fit: for the company, the candidate, and where things are headed in 18 months.

What I'm looking for

  • Product engineers who care about what they're building, not just the stack
  • Full-stack engineers comfortable shipping in small, fast-moving teams
  • People with startup pedigree — who've shipped, broken things, fixed them, repeated

What I'm not

Transactional. Spray-and-pray. The recruiter who sends your CV somewhere without calling you first. Not that one.

"The best engineers I've placed weren't looking. Getting those conversations right is the job."

The honest part

I don't have the engineering brain — I know this. But I have the obsession with getting something working, and it turns out that takes you further than you'd expect.

If I'd become an engineer, I'd have been a product engineer. In this life I'm their recruiter, which means I've spent years watching how the best ones think. That's worth something too.
